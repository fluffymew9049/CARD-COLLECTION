Card Pictures Guide

To display real photos or artwork of your Naruto/Pokémon cards, you must paste direct image links into your Google Sheet under the IMAGE column.

1. Create the Column

In Row 1, name your column exactly: IMAGE.

2. Where to get Image Links
  • Online databases: Search up your card on TCG websites, right-click the card picture, and select "Copy Image Address".
  • Your own photos: Upload photos of your cards to a free host like Postimages.org, and copy the "Direct Link" ending in .jpg or .png.
